VERONICA — SCREENS v1
One direction, built from the plan's design rules: only #000 and #FFF. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, outline vs fill, and inversion. Doto is used for numbers and titles, Inter for everything else. Frosted glass appears only on the tab bar, sheets and widgets. All data is from mockdata.md. Glyph previews are a rough diagram of the Phone (2a) back: lit segments are solid, unlit ones dotted.
